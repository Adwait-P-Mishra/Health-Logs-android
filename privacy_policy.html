Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

Welcome to Gym Logs. Your privacy is critically important to us. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Gym Logs is designed to be a local-first application. This means:

  • Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification (such as your name or phone number) to use the app.
  • Health and Fitness Data: The app allows you to log workout exercises, sets, weights, and repetitions. It also allows you to log meals, calories, and macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat).
  • Local Storage: All data you enter into Gym Logs is stored locally on your device using a private database. We do not upload your fitness or nutrition logs to external servers.

2. Use of Information

The information stored in the app is used solely to provide you with tracking features, such as:

  • Displaying your workout and meal history.
  • Calculating daily nutritional totals.
  • Visualizing your progress over time.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of Gym Logs does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or cloud synchronization services. Your data remains entirely under your control on your physical device.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, it remains there until you choose to delete it. You can delete individual logs within the app or clear all data by uninstalling the application or clearing the app cache/storage in your device settings.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
